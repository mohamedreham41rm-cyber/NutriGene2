# NutriGene

### AI-Powered Context-Aware Nutrition

NutriGene is an AI-powered nutrition application designed to help people make more informed food choices based on their individual health profiles.

## The Problem

Food labels can be difficult to interpret. Generic nutrition information does not always account for individual health conditions, dietary restrictions, or specific nutritional needs.

## Our Solution

NutriGene aims to provide personalized food product analysis by interpreting ingredient lists in the context of each user's health profile.

Instead of evaluating ingredients in isolation, the application considers the complete ingredient list alongside relevant health conditions and dietary restrictions.

## Key Features

- Context-aware food product analysis.
- Personalized food suitability assessment.
- Nutrition and ingredient interpretation.
- Health-profile-based recommendations.
- Clear explanations to support informed food choices.

## Technology

The initial application was developed using Base44.

The project combines nutrition knowledge, structured rules, and AI-powered interpretation to support personalized food analysis.

## Project Status

NutriGene is an evolving project. This repository contains selected project files and configuration information.

## Vision

Our vision is to make personalized nutrition more accessible by helping people understand what is in their food and how it relates to their individual health needs.
